AI Video Assistant

Turns any YouTube video or audio file into a transcript, a structured summary, action items, decisions, and open questions — plus a chatbot you can question about its contents. Audio is chunked with yt-dlp/ffmpeg, transcribed through three switchable engines (Deepgram, Sarvam, and local Whisper), summarised map-reduce style with Mistral so long recordings never blow the context window, and indexed into ChromaDB for retrieval-augmented Q&A. Every run is persisted, so reopening a video is instant.
